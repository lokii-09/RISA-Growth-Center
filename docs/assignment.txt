Growth  APM  Assignment  
Imagine  you're  responsible  for  driving  the  success  of  a  major  college  festival  in  a  tier  1  city.  You  have  limited  time,  
budget,
 
and
 
resources,
 
but
 
an
 
ambitious
 
goal.
 Choose  a  Tier  1  Indian  city  for  the  festival  and  briefly  justify  your  
preference.
 
 
Choose  one  of  two  challenges:  
●  Challenge  A   -   Attendee  Growth:  Maximize  the  number  of  people  who  actually  attend  the  festival.  ●  Challenge  B   -   Sponsorship  Growth:  Maximize  the  sponsorship  cash  collected  before  the  festival.  
We  want  to  see  how  you  set  ambitious  goals,  make  decisions  with  incomplete  information,  prioritize  opportunities,  and  
adapt
 
based
 
on
 
results.
 
Part  1   -   Your  Growth  Strategy  (Exactly  3  Slides)  
Slide  1   -   How  High  Can  You  Aim?  
What's  the  highest  outcome  you  believe  you  can  achieve,  and  why  should  we  believe  your  number?  
Build  a  quantitative  model  supporting  your  target.  
Attendee  Growth:  
●  Identify  target  audiences,  their  potential  size,  and  why  you'd  prioritize  them.  ●  Model  the  funnel  from  reach  →  registrations  →  actual  attendees.  ●  Estimate  conversions,  acquisition  costs,  and  capacity  constraints.  ●  Present  a  realistic  base  case  and  an  ambitious  stretch  target.  
Sponsorship  Growth:  
●  Identify  potential  sponsors,  their  motivations,  and  your  selection  criteria.  ●  Model  the  funnel  from  prospects  →  conversations  →  proposals  →  signed  deals  →  cash  collected.  ●  Estimate  conversions,  deal  sizes,  timelines,  and  collectible  cash.  ●  Present  a  realistic  base  case  and  an  ambitious  stretch  target.  We're  evaluating: Ambition,  evidence- based  assumptions,  accurate  calculations,  and  confidence  in  your  reasoning.  Slide  2   -   Where  Will  You  Place  Your  Bets?  
If  you  could  pursue  five  ideas  but  had  limited  resources,  where  would  you  invest,  and  what  would  you  avoid?  
Propose  and  rank  five  distinct  strategies.  
For  each  idea,  explain:  
●  The  concept  and  why  it  could  work.  ●  Expected  incremental  contribution  to  your  target.  ●  Budget,  resources,  and  execution  timeline.  ●  How  you  would  test  it  and  which  KPIs  you  would  monitor.  
Use  a  prioritization  framework  of  your  choice.  Consider  constraints,  dependencies,  risks,  and  overlap  between  ideas.

 Make  your  bets  explicit: Which  ideas  would  you  double  down  on?  Which  would  you  deprioritize  or  reject,  and  why?  We're  evaluating: Creativity,  prioritization,  understanding  of  tradeoffs,  and  the  ability  to  make  deliberate  choices.  Slide  3   -   How  Will  You  Know  What's  Working?  
What  will  determine  the  success  or  failure  of  your  ideas,  and  how  will  you  adapt  before  it's  too  late?  
For  each  idea:  
●  Identify  the  critical  variables  and  biggest  risks.  ●  Define  early  signals  of  success  or  failure.  ●  Set  measurable  thresholds  to  scale,  change,  or  stop.  ●  Explain  the  actions  you'd  take  when  performance  changes.  ●  Show  how  your  dashboard  and  AI  tools  would  help  you  make  these  decisions.  We're  evaluating: Understanding  of  cause  and  effect,  experimentation,  leading  indicators,  and  the  ability  to  respond  to  
evidence.
  
Part  2   -   Build  Your  Growth  Center  
Your  strategy  shouldn't  live  only  in  a  presentation.  
Build  a  working  analytics  dashboard,  campaign  management  tool,  and  embedded  AI  chatbot  for  your  chosen  challenge.  
Think  of  this  as  the  command  center  your  team  would  use  to  run,  monitor,  and  continuously  optimize  the  festival  
campaign.
 1.  Measure  Performance  
Track  the  complete  growth  funnel,  campaign- level  KPIs,  targets  versus  actuals,  conversion  rates,  budgets,  and  projected  
outcomes.
 2.  Diagnose  Performance  
Identify  which  campaigns  are  performing  well,  which  are  underperforming,  where  bottlenecks  exist,  and  what  is  driving  
changes.
 3.  Support  Decisions  
Help  the  team  prioritize  initiatives,  allocate  resources,  and  decide  what  to  scale,  change,  or  stop  based  on  performance.  4.  Manage  Execution  
Enable  the  team  to  manage  campaigns,  tasks,  owners,  deadlines,  and  action  items.  Changes  to  the  underlying  data  
should
 
update
 
the
 
relevant
 
analytics.

What  We  Look  For  
We  evaluate  both  the  quality  of  your  thinking  and  how  well  your  product  turns  insights  into  action.   
Weight  Evaluation  focus  
20%  Ambition  and  quantitative  reasoning. Evidence- backed  growth  targets,  audience  or  sponsor  selection,  funnel  math,  realistic  assumptions,  and  stretch  potential.  
20%  Creative  ideas,  prioritization  and  tradeoffs. Five  differentiated  ideas,  informed  bets,  realistic  constraints,  contribution  estimates,  and  clear  reasons  to  deprioritize  alternatives.  
15%  Experimentation  and  adaptability. Leading  indicators,  clear  success  and  failure  signals,  measurable  scale/change/ stop  thresholds,  and  sensible  next  actions.  
20%  Dashboard  and  management  functionality. Accurate,  updated  funnel  analytics,  usable  campaign  and  task  tracking,  saved  changes,  and  a  functioning  insight- to- action  workflow.  
20%  Embedded  AI  chatbot  utility. Grounded  answers  using  current  data,  reliable  calculations,  useful  diagnosis  and  recommendations,  execution  support,  and  honest  handling  of  uncertainty.  
5%  Ownership  and  communication. Clear  explanation  of  your  decisions,  implementation,  tools,  data  sources,  testing,  and  limitations.  A  polished  UI  or  large  projected  number  alone  will  not  earn  a  high  score.  We  may  test  your  product  by  changing
